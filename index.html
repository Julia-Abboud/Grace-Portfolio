Meet the Designer

Drawn to the details that give clothing character, Grace approaches fashion as both storytelling and art. Her work plays with proportion, texture, movement, and the unexpected to explore ideas ranging from fashion’s relationship with climate change to the evolving intersection of art and technology.

With more than a decade spent designing and creating, Grace’s work has traveled across the country and appeared in exhibitions including at The Met. Her creative practice is shaped by professional internships, volunteer work, independent projects, and a constant curiosity for what clothing can communicate.

Thank you for taking the time to explore her work.
