Meet the Artist Grace A. Abboud is a fashion design student pursuing a BFA in Fashion Design at the Fashion Institute of Technology in New York.

Her work sits at the intersection of silhouette, material, and movement, with an interest in turning ideas into garments that feel considered from every angle.
